Welcome to Golden Memory

Bengal’s craft, designed for your celebrations

Golden Memory was founded by Gouranga Kuila with one idea: bring the beauty of Bengal’s traditional crafts into modern homes and celebrations. From grand wedding stages and Durga Puja theme décor to delicate jute idols and vases, every piece is designed and made by our own team.

We work from our Kolkata office at Rajdanga and our workshop in Nandakumar, Purba Medinipur.

  • Wedding & reception décor
  • Durga Puja theme décor
  • Handmade jute idols & vases
  • Custom gifts & home décor
